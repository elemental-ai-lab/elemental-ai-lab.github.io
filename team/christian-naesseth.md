My research develops the foundations of generative modelling and probabilistic inference, with applications across the sciences and engineering. I was previously a postdoctoral research scientist with David Blei at Columbia University, and did my PhD in electrical engineering, specialising in automatic control, at Linköping University with Fredrik Lindsten and Thomas Schön. I am an ELLIS Scholar; my thesis received the Savage Award from the International Society for Bayesian Analysis, and my work has received best paper awards at AISTATS and AABI.
